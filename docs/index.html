What Problem Does This Solve?

📰 Real Story: The Music Fraud That Shocked the Industry

In 2024, an American musician named Michael Smith was charged with wire fraud for creating over 1,000 fake "ghost" artist accounts on Spotify. He used AI tools to generate thousands of ambient and relaxation tracks, uploaded them under fake names, then used bots to stream them millions of times — stealing over $10 million in royalties from real artists.

Music streaming platforms pay artists based on how many times their songs are played. If someone can create a fake artist, upload lots of songs, and generate fake streams — they steal money from the pool that real artists should receive.

This project builds a detection system that analyzes music catalog data and flags artists that look suspiciously like ghost factories.

In one sentence: GhostTrack looks at 7 different clues about a music artist and decides whether they're a real musician or a fraud bot generating fake songs for money.
